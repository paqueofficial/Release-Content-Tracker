Content Tracker

Brainstorm and track the social content around a release — idea by idea, grouped by track, from first thought to posted.

How it works

  1. Name your project, then add ideas one at a time. Put the track name each idea belongs to — type the same name again to keep stacking ideas under that track.
  2. Write the idea in your own words — loose is fine, like "walking down the street, performance shoot with friends in the background." Pick a format and set where it is: idea, to shoot, shot, edited or posted.
  3. Rate each idea two ways. Effort is how much work it takes to make — filming, editing, coordinating people (1 easy, 5 hard). Reach is how far it's likely to travel — roughly how many people it could get in front of, based on the hook, format and trend (1 low, 5 high). Sort quick wins first to surface the low-effort, high-reach ideas, or sort by what you're filming next. Add a shoot date and draft the caption too — then saving clears the form for the next idea.
  4. Save the session as a file (.json) to keep or reopen anywhere, or export to Word, PDF or Markdown. The page also auto-saves to this browser — handy, but not a real backup.
